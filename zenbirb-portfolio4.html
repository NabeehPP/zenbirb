About ZENBIRB

I'm Nafih, creating as ZENBIRB. I explore emerging Web3 projects, DeFi protocols, and crypto ecosystems, then turn that research into clear, useful content.

The work focuses on explaining products, covering narratives, and participating in creator campaigns that help people understand what's being built.

Based in India. Also building in design and marketing through a studio practice. Always interested in connecting with builders and other creators.

Focus areas

  • 03 Research, alpha drops, and narrative coverage
  • 04 Creator campaigns and community initiatives

How the promotional process works

A proposed workflow for collaborations. Adapted to each project's needs. No guaranteed outcomes.

01

Discover

Understand the project's product, mission, audience, ecosystem, and key differentiators.

02

Research

Explore the product and identify the most interesting features, use cases, and stories worth communicating.

03

Create

Develop content suited to the project, such as X threads, explainers, campaign posts, or other agreed formats.

04

Publish

Publish the approved content through the relevant channels, with clear messaging and appropriate links.

05

Connect

Encourage meaningful discussion and help interested audiences discover the project's official resources.
